Our Conviction

The most valuable thing a financier can offer is certainty.

South Africa does not lack entrepreneurial energy; it lacks capital that behaves well. JODO exists so that good businesses are no longer lost to bad timing.

Most capital is patient in the brochure and impatient in the committee room. Founded in 2025, JODO was built on a different premise: that the most valuable thing a financier can offer a growing business is certainty.

Good businesses are lost to bad timing — a bridging gap measured in weeks, a contract award with no working capital behind it, a growth opportunity that dies while a large lender "circulates the paper." Every member of our team has watched that happen from the other side of the table. JODO exists so that it does not have to.

We are boutique by design. Deliberately small means deliberately accountable: the person who underwrites your transaction is the person whose name is on it. Our investors get the same bargain — direct access to the people making the decisions, structures explained in plain language, and economics that are transparent enough to be argued with.

Our Ambition

We intend to build a homegrown South African institution — rooted here, regulated here, invested here.

Institutions are not built by headcount. They are built by kept promises, compounding quietly over years.

Why the Name Matters

Technique, timing and redirection.

Read JODO beside its martial-arts homophone and the philosophy announces itself. Judo teaches that raw force is rarely the winning argument — technique, timing and redirection are. Markets reward the same physics.

Credit constraint, dislocation and confusion at scale are enormous forces; we are built to receive them and turn them into an advantage for the businesses we back and the investors who fund them. It is a philosophy of applied intelligence: capital, applied with intent.

Mission · Vision · Purpose

Not wall statements. Testable ones.

Each one is testable: our mission in the way a mandate is priced, our vision in who calls us first, and our purpose in what we refuse to finance.

Mission

To structure private credit, private equity and bespoke investment solutions that give ambitious businesses certainty of funding, and give investors disciplined, downside-aware returns — delivered through partnership, transparency and technical craft.

Vision

To be South Africa's most trusted boutique capital partner: the first call for founders, intermediaries and institutions when a transaction is difficult, urgent or unconventional — and the firm other firms measure themselves against.

Purpose

To prove that a disciplined, independent firm can out-think, out-serve and out-perform larger incumbents — while widening access to capital for the entrepreneurs and communities the mainstream economy overlooks.

Our Values

Four values, chosen because they are expensive to keep.

Any firm can print them; you will only know ours by what we decline.

Discipline

We say no faster than we say yes.

Every decline protects a yes that matters. We would rather be remembered for the deals we refused than for the ones we regret.

Alignment

"Partners" is a promise, not a courtesy title.

It is in our name and in our economics: we sit on the same side of the table as our investors, and structure transactions so our clients' wins are our wins.

Ingenuity

Where others see stalemate, we look for structure.

Difficult is our natural habitat. The best transactions we do are the ones that required a design no one else was willing to attempt.

Stewardship

Capital is trust in transit.

On every mandate, somebody's retirement and somebody's payroll are on the same side of the ledger. Both are treated as non-negotiable.
