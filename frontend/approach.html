How We Underwrite

Five principles. Applied without exception.

Our investment philosophy fits on one page because it has to survive contact with a credit committee.

01

Preservation precedes performance.

02

Cash flows must pay their own way.

03

Downside protection is engineered, not hoped for.

04

Returns are pursued, never promised.

05

We stay inside the perimeter of what we truly understand.

Our first question is never "how much can we make?" It is "how do we get the capital back?" We favour self-liquidating instruments repaid from the transaction's own cash flows. Security, covenants, sequencing and structure are negotiated before pricing is. We publish objectives, not guarantees — every return expectation is traced to identifiable cash flows or exits. And we stay inside a defined circle of competence, expanding it only through knowledge and experience, never appetite.

Alongside our own capital and balance sheet, we build coalitions for larger or transformational transactions: development finance institutions, pension funds, insurers and family offices whose mandates align with ours. Partnership is how a boutique firm punches above its weight.

The Boutique Difference

We win on what a large balance sheet cannot easily buy.

We compete against much larger balance sheets. We win on the things a large balance sheet cannot easily buy: speed, seniority, alignment and design.

The Institutional Machine

Credit decisions measured in committee cycles and quarters.

Access through layers of relationship managers who rotate.

Product-first origination: a standard menu looking for a client.

Returns described in narratives and league tables.

Compliance as a department at the end of the corridor.

Alignment asserted in the pitch deck.

The JODO Way

A mandate sized to decide at the speed of the opportunity — because in bridging and contract finance, time is the collateral.

A direct line to the people who underwrite the deal and answer for it.

Fit-first underwriting: the structure is designed around the transaction — or we decline it.

Returns defined by structure and process — every expectation traced to cash flows or exits, and stress-tested.

Regulation as design: dual-licensed — FSP 55333 and NCR 29734 under one roof.

Alignment practised: transparent economics and a habit of investing alongside our clients.

The result is a firm that behaves like a principal even when it is acting as an adviser, and like a partner even when it could simply be a lender. In a market where capital is abundant but certainty is scarce, certainty is the product.
