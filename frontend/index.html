The Firm at a Glance

A boutique investment and credit firm with a demanding purpose.

We operate at the intersection of two disciplines that are usually kept apart: the structuring rigour of institutional asset management and the speed and intimacy of a boutique advisory practice. One licence lets us advise and manage investments; the other lets us extend credit.

Holding both under one roof is not an administrative detail — it is the business model. It means a funding problem can be met with a funding structure, decided by the same people who will stand behind it.

"Business as usual is the most expensive strategy in private markets. Ours is deliberately different — smaller, sharper, and accountable to the next generation of South African enterprise."

— JODO Capital Partners

How We Underwrite

Five principles. Applied without exception.

1. Preservation precedes performance.

Our first question is never "how much can we make?" It is "how do we get the capital back?"

2. Cash flows pay their own way.

We favour self-liquidating instruments repaid from the transaction's own cash flows.

3. Downside protection is engineered.

Security, covenants, sequencing and structure are negotiated before pricing is.
